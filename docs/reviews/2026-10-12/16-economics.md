Carrier: four **G6K-2P-Y DC5**, five AO3400A, one AO3401A, four **1N4148W-E3-08**, eleven resistors/four headers. Supervisor: 14 ICs, BAS70-05-7-F, 24 resistors, 19 capacitors/six headers.
