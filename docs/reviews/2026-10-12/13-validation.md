# 13 — Physical validation and HIL

**Verdict: PROTOTYPE FIRST, isolated bench only.** Continue one decisive transfer experiment before further architecture expansion. No physical operation or Nissan acceptance is established. Labels below distinguish source evidence from proposals; this review performed no hardware test.

1. **CONFIRMED SOURCE — acceptance is entirely pending.** `hardware/review_evidence/README.md:29–36` and `validation-summary-final.json:65–83` record 34 NOT_RUN procedures, 33 overlapping PENDING probes and zero physical results. A passing Boolean/CAD check cannot justify energizing an unknown endpoint. `docs/passive-bypass-validation.csv:6–10,29–30` requires undefined channel-error, correlation and response limits. Separate reproducible bench screening from future application acceptance; missing limits block vehicle advancement, not bounded characterization.

2. **CONFIRMED SOURCE / INFERENCE — contact-resistance acceptance conflicts with fixture scope.** V01 (`docs/passive-bypass-validation.csv:3`) invokes supplier resistance limits, while V00 (`:2`) caps signals at 1 mA. [G6K datasheet, printed p.3, Characteristics footnote 1](https://omronfs.omron.com/en_US/ecb/products/pdf/en-g6k.pdf) measures its 100 mΩ initial limit at 10 mA/1 V. An ordinary continuity meter has unspecified test energy; neither it nor a 1 mA reading reproduces that specification. Characterize bounded low-current path voltage/error with stated uncertainty; authorize any separate supplier-method component test separately. Do not silently increase fixture current or claim supplier equivalence.

3. **CONFIRMED SOURCE / INFERENCE — instrumentation is underspecified.** `fixture_bom.csv:9` says “suitable channels”; V04/V05 require correlated waveforms. A 1 MΩ probe changes the nominal 10 kΩ/100 kΩ, 5 V divider by about 41 mV; even 10 MΩ changes it about 4 mV. Include measured probe impedance/capacitance. Earth-referenced scope/USB grounds can defeat the deliberate reference-break experiment (V13, `validation.csv:15`). Verify isolated measurement connections before fault injection.

4. **CONFIRMED SOURCE — finite NC return can conceal a decisive failure.** `docs/passive-bypass-design.md:250–256` admits DAC collapse before contact release; local flyback slows release (`:132–134`). Measure the dead-selected-source interval and bounce, not merely final continuity. Do not apply the relay's catalog 3 ms as an assembled clamp/coil/bounce bound.

5. **CONFIRMED SOURCE — fault coverage must include successful reconnection that is undesirable.** V31 versus V32 (`validation.csv:33–34`) distinguishes complete bundle loss from a single request/permit wire. Reenergization with feedback intact is an expected coverage limitation, not proof of a fresh-arm guarantee. V27 (`:29`) is still an unidentified ECU simulator; HIL cannot begin from invented Nissan diagnostic tolerances.

**PROPOSED fastest decisive experiment.** First use one exact G6K/driver/flyback two-pole coupon if full boards are unavailable; it resolves transfer physics only. Independently maintained default sources and two actual MCP4728 outputs feed four 10 kΩ containment resistors, with 100 kΩ COM loads. Start at distinct 1–4 V levels; verify every source/fault stays ≤1 mA, temperature 20–30°C and coil-supply limits. Then repeat on assembled Rev A plus supervisor, verified harness, synthetic 30 ms WDI and manual READY/ARM.

Capture simultaneously COM-A, COM-B, actual DAC rail and coil current using four analog channels; digital channels capture stimulus, PERMIT, ARMED and HEALTH_N. Separate captures add differential Q5 VGS and returned supply. Use ≥1 MS/s mechanical records; separately use appropriately fast, low-capacitance probing for qualification glitches. Remove total power, 3.3 V only, DAC only, ENABLE and complete bundle; test 4.0–4.5 V dwell, short dips and single-wire return. Repeat 30 times per case across heartbeat phase, retaining every trace and extrema; this is screening, not reliability evidence.

**PROPOSED stop/revision gates:** stop on excess current, any unintended settled source connection, uncommanded pickup or complete-bundle recovery without fresh qualification/ARM. Investigate every missing NC return and inconsistent transfer. Record error and interruption duration without inventing a Nissan pass time. Advance to HIL only after endpoint identity, measured impedance, diagnostic/correlation regions and allowed response times exist; replay worst measured waveforms there before vehicle consideration.
