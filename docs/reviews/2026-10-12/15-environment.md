# 15 — EMC and vehicle environment

Independent first pass, 2026-10-12. Verdict: **PROTOTYPE FIRST**, limited to the stated isolated simulator fixture. **NO vehicle advancement.** Confidence: high in the scope gap; environmental robustness remains UNKNOWN. No physical tests or shared validation were performed by this specialist.

## Ranked findings

1. **Vehicle supply substituted for the regulated fixture → exceeded limits or corrupted supervision.** CONFIRMED SOURCE: `docs/passive-bypass-design.md:35–45` requires regulated 5 V, 20–30°C and current containment; `tools/build_passive_bypass_board.py:124–129` connects flyback diodes and Q5 directly to the coil-feed structure. [AOS AO3400A, p.1](https://www.aosmd.com/sites/default/files/res/datasheets/AO3400A.pdf) and [AO3401A, p.1](https://www.aosmd.com/sites/default/files/res/datasheets/AO3401A.pdf) specify 30 V drain-source magnitude and ±12 V gate-source absolute limits. These are limits, not protection performance. PROPOSED CHANGE: keep Rev A unchanged for bench investigation; specify a separate power/interface revision only after measuring the actual LEAF low-voltage supply and selecting its required profile. Do not copy an alternator/load-dump profile blindly onto an EV. [ISO 16750-2:2023 §4.2–4.10](https://www.iso.org/obp/ui#iso:std:iso:16750:-2:ed-5:v1:en) covers supply variations, polarity, ground offset, opens and shorts; [ISO 7637-2:2011, scope](https://www.iso.org/standard/50925.html) addresses supply-line transients independently of propulsion type. Neither supplies the missing application-specific acceptance envelope here.

2. **Ground offset or noisy interboard cable → false request/ARM/health state.** CONFIRMED SOURCE: `docs/passive-bypass-design.md:265–285` requires two ground destinations and returned 5 V sense; supervisor `README.md:70–74` specifies short local logic wires and minimum loaded HIGH. ANALYTICAL INFERENCE: remote grounding/harness substitution invalidates that local-wire assumption; filtered carrier-sense recognition can miss brief chatter (`README.md:84`). Measure permission, latch state, loaded GPIO/gate voltage, ground differential and coil current together. Include complete ground loss and individual conductors, with stimuli contained within verified pin limits. [ISO 7637-3:2016, scope](https://www.iso.org/standard/59603.html) specifically covers coupling onto non-supply lines. A protected supply alone cannot establish interface immunity.

3. **ESD/RF disturbance at exposed headers → false arming, reset or damaged interface.** CONFIRMED SOURCE: bench headers are explicitly exposed (`docs/passive-bypass-design.md:278–280`). [TPS3430-Q1, pp.1,5](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps3430-q1.pdf) distinguishes automotive qualification/component HBM/CDM ratings; these do not demonstrate assembled-board ESD behavior. Later interface testing needs selected [ISO 10605:2023](https://www.iso.org/standard/79094.html) ESD methods, [ISO 11452-2:2019](https://www.iso.org/standard/68557.html) RF immunity and [CISPR 25:2021](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/64645) receiver-protection emissions assessment. UNKNOWN: levels, harness configuration and permitted functional degradation. Do not claim compliance from public abstracts.

4. **Temperature, vibration or aging → failed transfer/default continuity.** CONFIRMED SOURCE: room-temperature calculations explicitly exclude automotive-temperature budgeting (`design.md:167–173`); no environmental results exist (`:387–397`). [G6K legacy manufacturer sheet, p.3](https://omronfs.omron.com/en_US/ecb/products/pdf/en-g6k.pdf) gives condition-dependent durability, vibration and non-condensing limits; newer manufacturer listings differ in temperature range. Reconcile purchased suffix/revision before adopting any range. Component figures do not qualify solder, headers, enclosure or signal continuity. Define mounting location, duty cycle, service life and maintenance before selecting [ISO 16750-3:2023](https://www.iso.org/standard/77579.html) mechanical and [16750-4:2023](https://www.iso.org/standard/77580.html) climatic tests.

## Stage gates and decisive experiment

Bench gate: inspect assembly/cable mapping, current containment and all unpowered NC paths first. Then simultaneously capture both selected channels, coil current, supply/ground and permission during slow/fast rail loss and connector interruption; repeat at stated bench corners with installed diodes. Record glitches and stable-NC return; application timing limits remain UNKNOWN. Stop for unexpected current, unintended energization/rearm, lost bypass continuity or rating violations.

Vehicle gate: identified harness/functions, measured envelope and acceptable response limits, mounting/life profile, protection/interface design, instrumented environmental evidence and independent engineering acceptance. A benign bench result cannot waive these gates.
