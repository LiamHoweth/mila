# Debate 06 — PCB/fabrication ↔ connectors/human factors

**Actual exchange:** PCB specialist challenged the connectors specialist on whether adapters suffice before a new board, parallel/hidden ground returns, switch sequencing, reconnect recovery and export provenance. Connectors responded; PCB accepted its recovery/ground corrections and requested export clarification; connectors explicitly confirmed the bounded adapter proposal and metadata gate. No new CAD was generated.

**Accepted improvement:** Freeze Rev A and build a retained, polarized adapter/breakout fixture with photographed function mapping, endpoint labels, insulation, strain relief and 100%-net continuity acceptance. Changing the PCB headers immediately has no demonstrated benefit over a correctly retained adapter for the first isolated experiment. Revise hardware if the physical adapter still permits reversal/offset insertion or measurements identify unacceptable disturbance. The fixture itself needs fit/retention inspection; drawings alone do not prove that improvement.

**Accepted correction to PCB emphasis:** Carrier routed GND/no copper plane is an UNKNOWN transient margin, not a confirmed electrical fault. The concrete ground-fault issue is that supervisor J5-1 branches to both JPWR1-2 and JCTRL1-5, which share carrier GND (`docs/passive-bypass-design.md:266–276`). These are parallel returns rather than independently supervised returns. Opening one while the other, USB, CAN or instrument ground remains connected need not disarm and does not test total reference loss. Enumerate actual returns; use suitably isolated differential instruments.

**Accepted recovery limitation:** No universal “reconnect requires ARM” claim survives. A complete bundle loss clears ARM only after feedback/health recognition; a request/permission wire can open/rejoin with feedback valid and restore an old selection (`docs/passive-bypass-design.md:282–288`). Both specialists accept prohibiting powered harness service in this bench procedure. This procedural limit does not add hardware fault detection.

**Resolving experiment:** After power-off terminal/continuity checks, use the current-limited fixture. Capture physical coil feed, auxiliary DEADMAN, returned feedback/SENSE, ARMED/permit, coil current and COM signals during actual ENABLE release. ENABLE pole timing is nonsimultaneous, so neither auxiliary status nor ARMED proves contact transfer. Perform ≥100 ms complete-bundle interruptions, shorter-pulse sweeps, individual asserted-request/permit open/rejoins, each GND branch loss, and both/upstream return loss. Explicitly control alternate USB/instrument returns. Record recognized outage duration, resumed selections, reference offsets and actual NC restoration; stop on unintended energization/backfeed or uncontrolled source joining.

**Accepted fabrication boundary:** Future orders require exact BOM, finish/revision/placement conventions and complete frozen export-payload hashes. Clean CAD, source hashes and existing Gerber references are useful provenance, not assembler-ready approval. There is no evidence of stale exports or a confirmed land-pattern fault.

**Disagreement outcome:** No unresolved disagreement between the two specialists. Both reject any general guarantee that disconnect/reconnect forces rearming. Physical acceptance limits and retained-adapter effectiveness remain UNKNOWN pending the experiment. Neither endorses vehicle integration.
