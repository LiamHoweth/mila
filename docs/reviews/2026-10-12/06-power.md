# 06 — Power and partial power

Independent memo, 2026-10-12. **Verdict: PROTOTYPE FIRST**, with isolated simulator supplies; no vehicle or dual-source ESP connection approval. The topology is credible enough to measure, but partial-power inhibition and release timing remain UNKNOWN. No physical result is present in the reviewed acceptance records.

## Ranked findings

1. **Partial 3.3 V can escape endpoint reasoning.** With 5 V live and 3.3 V slowly falling/rising, U13 traverses voltages outside its specified operating range; Q6 can amplify a small gate excursion into Q5 conduction. CONFIRMED SOURCE: supervisor `pin_net.csv` connects U13 to V3V3; carrier `passive_bypass.net` connects Q6 drain to Q5 gate, biased by RPG1=10 kΩ. [TI LVC1G08-Q1 Rev H, §§5.3/5.5, pp.5–6](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc1g08-q1.pdf) specifies operation ≥1.65 V and Ioff at VCC=0, not every intermediate voltage. ANALYTICAL INFERENCE: two 82 kΩ pulldowns limit worst-case endpoint leakage to approximately 0.42 V, but [AO3400A, p.2](https://www.aosmd.com/sites/default/files/res/datasheets/AO3400A.pdf) gives threshold at 250 µA, not an OFF-current bound at 0.42 V. Measure coil current and contact selection, not gate voltage alone.

2. **An alternate downstream supply defeats electronic and physical cutoff.** Carrier netlist confirms Q5 source=5V_RAW, drain=COIL_5V; its body diode conducts from externally raised COIL_5V toward 5V_RAW. [AO3401A, pp.1–3](https://www.aosmd.com/sites/default/files/res/datasheets/AO3401A.pdf) confirms the single P-channel/body-diode structure. CONFIRMED SOURCE: only relay coils, clamp cathodes and Q5 drain occupy COIL_5V. PROPOSED CHANGE: preserve that exclusive feed and document every USB/DAC/instrument source. Reverse-current blocking becomes a redesign requirement if another source is unavoidable.

3. **PG5 improves detection but is neither instant nor DAC supervision.** R22=100 kΩ/R23=9.53 kΩ senses returned pre-Q5 carrier input; C17=1 nF gives approximately 8.7 µs RC. [TPS3808-Q1 Rev J, §§7.3–7.6/8.3, pp.4–6/9–10](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps3808-q1.pdf) supports nominal 4.655 V, approximately 4.55–4.76 V falling threshold with stated tolerances, and ≤VDD SENSE requirement. Short chatter can evade recognition; a separate DAC-power conductor can fail while feedback stays valid. Do not assign a vehicle response limit to these numbers.

4. **The diode OR solves live-rail ordering, not all backfeed cases.** Supervisor netlist confirms BAS70-05 common cathode supplying U3, with separate V3V3/V5_SUP anodes. This preserves U3 power when either normal rail disappears. [BAS70 datasheet, pp.1–2](https://www.diodes.com/datasheet/download/BAS70.pdf) supplies topology and room-temperature leakage/forward-drop limits; it does not prove zero reverse current. Measure off-rail rise and U3 supply across independent ramps and high-impedance disconnected supplies.

5. **Physical enable removes incoming energy; coils still contain energy.** Carrier D1–D4 form local recirculation paths even after the upstream feed opens. [TE suppression guidance, “The Effect of Coil Suppression”](https://www.te.com/en/products/relays-and-contactors/electromechanical-relays/intersection/relay-coil-suppression-dc-relays.html) explains delayed release. Coil inductance and assembled decay are UNKNOWN. Test Q5 cutoff with low-side short, and physical opening with Q5 forced on; measure every COM through final NC settling.

6. **USB/V5 protection is board-specific UNKNOWN.** PROJECT_CONTEXT records a 38-pin WROOM-32D board, not its verified revision. [Espressif DevKitC V4 power options](https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-dev-kits/en/latest/esp32/esp32-devkitc/user_guide.html#power-supply-options) requires mutually exclusive sources for that board; it is not proof this board matches. Identify regulator/diode markings and trace USB-V5-3V3 before powered-controller tests.

## Decisive experiment and stop criteria

Execute pending S13–S16/S19/S21/S25/S27 using fixture-generated control first: independent fast steps and slow ramps, held ARM, requests high, complete harness, differential gate probes, coil-current shunt and all COM captures. Stop on unintended contact selection, sustaining downstream feed after enable release, absolute-limit excursion, or automatic rearm. Record current tails and interruption intervals; accept vehicle timing only after an application limit exists. Add protection or faster suppression only when measured failures justify it.
