## Ranked findings

1. **P1 — Harness misassembly can defeat the design or apply 5 V to logic.** Carrier JCTRL1 is six pins; supervisor J5 is seven pins in a different functional order. JCTRL1-6/J5-7 carries returned 5V_RAW, adjacent to carrier GND, rather than a GPIO signal (`hardware/passive_bypass_rev_a/pin_net_map.csv:82–105`; supervisor `README.md:57–74`). J4B1/JDAC1 also change the legacy physical pinout. Unkeyed headers are a confirmed assembly hazard, not a proven electrical failure. PROPOSED CHANGE: a numbered, function-to-function harness drawing with photographs, retained mating orientation and measured 100%-net continuity; insulated strain relief and dedicated breakout fixture before powering. Do not assume matching connector pin numbers.

2. **P1 — CAD connectivity cannot establish rail/ground transient margins.** Carrier GND is routed rather than poured; supervisor uses a bottom plane with many routing cuts (`mila_passive_bypass_supervisor_rev_a.kicad_pcb:28516–28534`). Its decouplers are about 1.89–2.51 mm from power pins (`pcb_validation/routing_summary.json:2000–2092`). This is not evidence of a bad return path. UNKNOWN: actual disturbance at U1/U10/U11/U13 and shared controller ground during all-coil switching and cable removal. Scope local VDD-to-local-GND and COIL_PERMIT simultaneously; require no unintended permission pulse or arming. [TI TPS3430-Q1](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps3430-q1.pdf), §10, p29, requires low-impedance supply/nearby bypassing. Digital nets and geometric clock skew alone do not prove margin.

3. **P2 — Supplier-ready release provenance is incomplete.** Main checkpoint hashes CAD/BOM/positions but does not individually hash Gerber/drill payloads (`validation-summary-final.json:85–144`). Both jobs say revision `rev?` and finish `None` (supervisor `manufacture/*-job.gbrjob:10–23`; carrier equivalent). This does not prove stale exports. Before ordering, freeze a complete export manifest, explicit finish/stackup/tolerances, exact purchased BOM and assembler placement-origin/rotation convention; inspect copper/drill overlays against the frozen board. Do not send an unlabeled mixture of snapshots.

4. **P2 — Assembly process needs evidence, especially U1 and polarized relays.** Corrected relay pad rows 0/3.2/5.4/7.6 mm, 5.08 mm separation and 0.85 mm holes agree with [Omron G6K](https://omronfs.omron.com/en_US/ecb/products/pdf/en-g6k.pdf), G6K-2P-Y drawing, p6 (`MiLa_Bench.pretty/Relay_G6K_2P_Y_corrected.kicad_mod:299–360`). U1 has a 0.5 mm-pitch exposed-pad VSON footprint. Require real-part/1:1 footprint fit and polarized pinout verification; stencil/reflow capability and postassembly inspection. A passing DRC cannot prove the hidden ground-pad joint.

5. **P2 — Test access is incomplete for transfer claims.** J6 exposes status logic, not coil current or contact state (supervisor `README.md:64`). Use labeled breakout leads to every used COM/NC/NO and coil node; avoid repeatedly probing tiny pads. No immediate PCB revision is justified merely to improve instrumentation.

**Stop criteria:** any power-off continuity/polarity mismatch, ambiguous harness identity, unexpected permission, or unrepeatable transfer capture stops energization. Resolve these on the bench before spending on further miniaturization. The two boards occupy 78 cm² before cables; whole-system size benefit versus the actual legacy module is UNKNOWN.
