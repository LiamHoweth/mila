# 12 — CAN and openpilot independent review

2026-10-12. Verdict: **PROTOTYPE FIRST**, isolated communications/signal bench only; vehicle integration **NO**. Confidence high on inspected code, low on target-vehicle compatibility. No transmission, flash or physical test performed.

## Ranked findings

1. **Missing vehicle identity can invalidate the cheaper CAN alternative.** CONFIRMED SOURCE: current upstream lists LEAF **2018–23 with ProPILOT Assist**, not a blanket LEAF port ([CARS](https://github.com/commaai/opendbc/blob/390618eef8be4462aebdfddb534d289b23684a23/docs/CARS.md), Nissan row). [values.py](https://github.com/commaai/opendbc/blob/390618eef8be4462aebdfddb534d289b23684a23/opendbc/car/nissan/values.py#L36-L71) specifies Nissan A for LEAF, Nissan B for Altima and a distinct instrument-cluster LEAF variant. Local `PROJECT_CONTEXT.md:59–69,96–105` leaves year/trim/harness unresolved. Consequence: purchasing a harness or having a compatible DBC does not establish support. First record model year/market/ProPILOT configuration, ADAS location, harness label/revision and physical bus destinations.

2. **A valid heartbeat cannot grant actuation authority.** CONFIRMED SOURCE: [Nissan safety](https://github.com/commaai/opendbc/blob/390618eef8be4462aebdfddb534d289b23684a23/opendbc/safety/modes/nissan.h#L58-L113) checks steering and permits a bounded message/bus/length list; `0x5A5` is outside that list. An ESP directly wired to vehicle CAN transmits outside panda's gate. Local `PROJECT_CONTEXT.md:46–55` correctly identifies this boundary. Proposal: preserve direct comma/panda vehicle connection; do not rewrite, replay or inject downstream. Analog outputs are also outside panda's CAN safeguards and need their own application-specific limits/override evidence.

3. **“Disarmed” diagnostic is not passive capture.** CONFIRMED SOURCE: `firmware/comma4_can_bench/src/main.cpp:83–108` selects Normal mode before ARM. [Microchip MCP2515 DS20001801K](https://ww1.microchip.com/downloads/aemDocuments/documents/APID/ProductDocuments/DataSheets/MCP2515-Family-Data-Sheet-DS20001801K.pdf), §§10.3/10.5, printed pp.59–60: Normal mode generates ACK/error frames; Listen-Only suppresses these. Wrong bitrate can disturb a connected bus despite `armed=false`. PROPOSED CHANGE: separate verified silent receive-only build/fixture for any future passive capture. Existing firmware remains isolated-bench-only.

4. **STOP is a software queue gate, not proven bus silence.** CONFIRMED SOURCE: `main.cpp:122–126` only clears `armed`; `184–196` queues controller TX; `263–271` stops further submissions. ANALYTICAL INFERENCE: a previously queued/retrying frame can outlive jumper removal/timeout; MCP2515 §10.5/CANCTRL documents retry and abort controls. No hardware queue abort or transceiver inhibit is implemented here. Measure last physical frame versus disable under missing ACK, arbitration loss and reconnect; define an actual stop bound before claiming “immediate.”

5. **Native CAN can reduce analog scope, conditionally.** CONFIRMED SOURCE: [carcontroller.py](https://github.com/commaai/opendbc/blob/390618eef8be4462aebdfddb534d289b23684a23/opendbc/car/nissan/carcontroller.py#L22-L76) sends steering, cancellation and HUD; it has no acceleration/brake command generation. ANALYTICAL INFERENCE: an identified supported vehicle plus steering/stock-ACC research objective might remove analog steering substitution. Custom independent acceleration/braking or unsupported-vehicle research is not satisfied by this port. One MCP2515 channel is a bus node, not an inline two-channel gateway (`PROJECT_CONTEXT.md:109–131`); do not add a gateway without a requirement.

## Tests, unknowns and stop criteria

Root owns automated checks. Local `tools/comma_can_listener.py:43–65` and `tools/comma_dbc_listener.py:29–47` demonstrate reception/decoding only, not authority, freshness or fault response. UNKNOWN: actual oscillator/transceiver/voltage, physical termination, installed software revision, target support, `0x5A5` collisions and end-to-end latency. Upstream master was freshly resolved to `390618eef8be4462aebdfddb534d289b23684a23`; this is not installed-device evidence.

Kill/pivot: no vehicle connection with diagnostic Normal-mode firmware; no downstream ESP actuation presented as panda protected; pause analog-function investment if an identified supported native path already meets the research objective. Bench success cannot satisfy openpilot's [manual takeover and constrained trajectory requirements](https://github.com/commaai/openpilot/blob/master/docs/SAFETY.md).
