**Validation challenged audit:** optimized assertions and stale-report capture weaken future acceptance provenance, but do they invalidate today's accepted board? **Audit accepted correction:** no. **Agreed future release change:** before new capture/release, replace safety-critical `assert` guards with explicit rejection; bind newly executed reports to PCB, schematic, library, rule and tool digests; recheck that binding at capture. `tools/build_passive_bypass_supervisor_board.py:107–136,663,675` establishes the present guard/capture exposure. Demonstrate rejection of a changed input under ordinary and optimized Python and of stale-report capture in a disposable copy. These proposed repairs have not been implemented or tested. They do not require another PCB revision or postpone bounded physical characterization of unchanged Rev A.

**Validation challenged resistance acceptance:** V01 references supplier limits while V00 limits every fixture signal to ≤1 mA. Audit accepted that [G6K p.3, Characteristics footnote 1](https://omronfs.omron.com/en_US/ecb/products/pdf/en-g6k.pdf)'s 100 mΩ method at 10 mA/1 V is not reproduced. Proposed V01 wording: bounded low-current path voltage/error with measurement uncertainty, without supplier equivalence. Any separate component-level supplier-method test needs its own scope; the current review does not authorize it or modify the matrix.

**Audit challenged validation:** containment resistors and probes deliberately alter the endpoints. Validation accepted: 10 kΩ/100 kΩ transfers, DAC decay and loaded COM accuracy are fixture-specific; record actual source/load/probe resistance and capacitance, ground topology and loaded levels. Thirty phase-varied trials are screening, with all failures retained, never lifetime or diagnostic-coverage evidence.

**Agreed evidence gate:** preserve assembly identity, relay suffix/lot, calibrated instrument/settings record and raw per-trial traces; measure worst dead-DAC interval, coil decay, release/bounce and correlation. Better test planning cannot raise completed physical-validation status. Identified Nissan endpoints, measured electrical envelopes and actual permitted diagnostic/response limits must precede endpoint HIL acceptance; replay measured worst-case transfers there. This measurement resolves the remaining application uncertainty. No vehicle permission follows from this debate.
