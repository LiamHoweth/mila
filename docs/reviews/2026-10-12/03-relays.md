# 03 — Mechanical relay specialist

Date: 2026-10-12. Independent Phase 1 review; no other specialist memos read. **Verdict: PROTOTYPE FIRST**, limited to the isolated simulator fixture. Four compact nonlatching Form-C packages are a defensible way to preserve eventual passive connectivity. They do not establish uninterrupted transfer, contact-fault tolerance or vehicle acceptance.

**CONFIRMED SOURCE:** Carrier BOM specifies four **G6K-2P-Y DC5**, not G6KU, -2P without -Y, or -T1. Standard 5 V coil: 237 Ω and 21.1 mA nominal, ±10% at 23°C; pickup ≤80% rated voltage, release ≥10%; approximately 100 mW. Standard contacts: 1 A switching at 30 VDC, 2 A carry, Ag/Au-alloy bifurcated construction. These are separate limits, not an actuator rating. [Current manufacturer K106-E1, p.3, Ratings/Characteristics](https://components.omron.com/us-en/system/files/2026-05/datasheet_pdf/K106-E1.pdf), retrieved through indexed primary-source text; direct PDF retrieval currently redirects to an inaccessible Aratas endpoint.

## Ranked findings

1. **Power removed while DAC-selected → dead-DAC exposure before NC restoration.** Local D1–D4 plain flyback diodes recirculate coil current even after upstream feed opens (`docs/passive-bypass-design.md:117–160`). Manufacturer diode guidance explicitly identifies longer release; diode/Zener suppression can shorten it. The quoted 3 ms relay release excludes bounce and is not an assembled-system cutoff bound. **ANALYTICAL INFERENCE:** settled-state model fidelity cannot close this risk. Measure power loss, deliberate inhibit and low-side release separately. [PCB relay precautions, p.8, surge suppression](https://omronfs.omron.com/en_US/ecb/products/pdf/precautions_pcb.pdf); [manufacturer glossary, Release time](https://components.omron.com/eu-en/products/basic-knowledge/relays/glossary).

2. **Near-zero or long-dwell signal loads → uncertain contact resistance/reliability.** Catalog 10 μA at 10 mV is a P-level reference with a **50 Ω failure criterion**, measured at 120 operations/min; it is not a universal guaranteed minimum load or 100 mΩ guarantee. The latter is measured at 10 mA/1 V. Fixture 10 kΩ/100 kΩ loading and ≤1 mA cap therefore require an application-level measurement method; a supplier resistance test must be separately contained, not silently run through this fixture. Measure first-transfer-after-dwell at actual minimum energy before higher-current measurements can condition contacts. [K106-E1, p.3, footnotes 1/4](https://components.omron.com/us-en/system/files/2026-05/datasheet_pdf/K106-E1.pdf).

3. **Wrong suffix/orientation → bypass and coil drive fail despite valid nets.** Read-only footprint inspection confirms 0/3.2/5.4/7.6 mm longitudinal positions, 5.08 mm columns, 0.85 mm holes. Symbol/checker map coil 1+/8−; COM/NC/NO = 3/2/4 and 6/7/5. These match the manufacturer's G6K-2P-Y drawing; the corrected local NC row is appropriate. Still require actual-part marking, orientation-mark and unpowered continuity inspection before solder/power. [Manufacturer dimensions, p.6, bottom-view drawing](https://omronfs.omron.com/en_US/ecb/products/pdf/en-g6k.pdf); `MiLa_Bench.pretty/Relay_G6K_2P_Y_corrected.kicad_mod`, `tools/check_passive_bypass_design.py:136–160`.

4. **Welded NO, stuck mechanism or open NC → cutoff cannot restore the default path.** `docs/passive-bypass-fmea.md:94–95` and model `PoleFault` already acknowledge this. No contact-position feedback or forced-guided mechanism is demonstrated. Spare poles are ordinary contacts; they must not be credited as proof another pole opened. Emulate these faults on a simulator and record the actual limitation.

5. **Catalog life/environment extrapolated → unsupported mission-life claim.** Mechanical 50 million and electrical 100,000 operations have distinct stated test conditions. Current indexed catalog says −40…+85°C; the older linked PDF says +70°C. Reconcile purchased lot/revision before temperature expansion. UL/CSA/EN62368-1 listings establish no automotive-control approval. [K106-E1, p.3](https://components.omron.com/us-en/system/files/2026-05/datasheet_pdf/K106-E1.pdf); [manufacturer model/status page](https://www.aratas.com/us-en/products/relays/G6K).

**PROPOSED CHANGE:** Keep the relay topology; gate further integration on V01–V06/V21/V25/V26 measurements. Scope every COM, DAC rail and coil-current decay; record open interval, bounce, skew and stable NC return. Compare clamps only if measured exposure justifies it. **UNKNOWN:** endpoint limits, coil inductance, fitted-lot release distribution, dwell/endurance behavior. **PHYSICAL RESULT:** none reviewed. Stop for unexpected source coupling, failed default continuity, persistent chatter, fixture overcurrent or damaged driver/clamp; block vehicle advancement until application limits and qualification exist.
