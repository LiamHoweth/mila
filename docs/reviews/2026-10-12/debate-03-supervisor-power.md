# Debate 03 — Supervisor versus power

Actual exchange on October 12, 2026 between specialists 05 and 06; both read the other's independent memo. Both retain **PROTOTYPE FIRST, isolated simulator only**. Coordinator reports fresh automated checks passed; no physical result resolves the following questions.

**Accepted correction — favorable reset ordering:** Supervisor challenged an overly broad implication that every slow 3.3 V ramp necessarily defeats inhibition. Power accepted that PG33's minimum falling threshold, approximately 3.07×0.985=3.024 V, normally clears latches before the logic leaves its 3.0 V timing band. On a sufficiently slow monotonic rise, monitor delay and a new ARM event also inhibit restart. This is conditional analytical support, not a bound for every ramp rate. Abrupt collapse can traverse invalid supply regions before detection/propagation finishes; U13 behavior below 1.65 V is unspecified. Both accepted that the approximately 0.42 V endpoint gate screen establishes neither Q6 OFF current nor coil inactivity.

**Accepted correction — WDI has its own electrical contract:** Power accepted that request/header HIGH ≥2.60 V is insufficient for U1 WDI, which requires ≥0.8×VDD at its pin: 2.64 V at 3.3 V. R14=100 Ω/R10=10 kΩ nominally requires about 2.6664 V at the header merely to meet that level, before tolerance/input-current margin. U6/U10 can count transitions not guaranteed accepted by U1, whose minimum pulse width is 50 ns.

**Accepted narrowing — false HB_GOOD is not contact-transfer proof:** Power challenged equating edge counting with actuation. Supervisor accepted the additional conditions: live supplies/enable, QUALIFIED high, fresh ARM with requests LOW, then a request before WDO faults. Power accepted that deliberately scheduled events need no nanosecond coincidence; a potential interval lasting tens of milliseconds remains plausible. Its existence and actual coil/contact effect are **UNKNOWN**, not observed failures.

**Accepted timing limitation:** Short QUALIFIED lows can clear flip-flops while delayed HB_ONE_SAFE remains high. The documented ≥10 µs LOW and ≥1 ms post-recovery heartbeat gap conditionally prevent this; they do not cover arbitrary glitches.

**Discriminating tests:** First validate compliant full-amplitude 1 ms pulses, 30 ms falling-edge spacing and required recovery gaps. Then: (1) stable 5 V with slow/fast 3.3 V ramps/steps, held ARM and HIGH requests; (2) healthy rails with WDI amplitude/width sweeps, deliberately sequenced ARM/request; (3) short-clear/next-edge phase sweeps. Capture HEALTH_N, HB_GOOD, ARMED/PERMIT, Q6 gate, Q5 VGS, coil current and COM. Label boundary sweeps outside the firmware contract. Stop and preserve traces on unexpected current/contact selection or restart. No theoretical dissent remains; actual partial-power and pulse-recognition behavior requires instruments.
