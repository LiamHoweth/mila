## Ranked findings

1. **UNKNOWN, blocking:** Connecting an unidentified endpoint can turn the deenergized “default” into active drive. If J4-8/9 are motor rails and 18/19 motor terminals, 00/11 are opposite polarities and 01/10 may brake. A sensor pair could instead see invalid correlation. “Linear-actuator” in `docs/six_relay_mapping.csv:14–19` is an inference, not a rating or safe-state requirement. Identify the actual cable destinations and endpoint part numbers against authoritative wiring, then record passive connector continuity and the endpoint’s measured electrical envelope. Voltage alone cannot distinguish a sensor from a low-current actuator-driver command.

2. **ANALYTICAL INFERENCE:** Healthy settled mixed states join both outputs to one source; they do **not inherently short J4-8 to J4-9**. They increase source loading: two 100 kΩ loads become 50 kΩ, changing a 5 V/10 kΩ fixture output from 4.545 to 4.167 V (`docs/passive-bypass-design.md:47–52`). External load impedance is absent from the topology graph; “no source short” does not mean zero source current or benign endpoint response.

3. **CONFIRMED MODEL LIMIT / UNKNOWN PHYSICAL:** On common cutoff, unequal release times can produce either same-source state and open intervals; bounce adds repeated transfers. `tools/passive_bypass_model.py:334–340` assumes supplied NO-open/NC-settle times, not measured bounce. `tests/test_passive_bypass_model.py:479–484` demonstrates one skew example. One DPDT/control wire cannot guarantee synchronized contacts. [OMRON G6K datasheet](https://omronfs.omron.com/en_US/ecb/products/pdf/en-g6k.pdf), p.3 Characteristics, lists 3 ms initial release but no complete installed diode/bounce/skew guarantee; [TE suppression guidance](https://www.te.com/en/products/relays-and-contactors/electromechanical-relays/intersection/relay-coil-suppression-dc-relays.html) explains suppression-dependent release.

4. **ANALYTICAL FAULT:** One stuck-NO pole can leave a same-source state indefinitely after cutoff. A BOTH contact fault directly bridges sources (`tests/test_passive_bypass_model.py:388–394`); automatic coil inhibition cannot repair either physical fault. Preserve these separate fault categories.

## Minimum experiment and stop criteria

**PROPOSED:** Execute V01/V02/V03/V04/V05/V22 with distinct 0–5 V sources through 10 kΩ and 100 kΩ loads, all fault currents ≤1 mA. Simultaneously record both COMs, both sources, coil currents and cutoff; capture differential voltage, release order, floating duration and bounce. Exercise all four states, both transition directions, power loss and emulated stuck poles. Instrument loading and fixture dividers must enter predictions.

Stop for wrong mapping, unexpected rail bridging, fixture current breach or unexplained reengagement. Vehicle advancement stays blocked until endpoint-specific allowable states, interruption/correlation limits and fault response are defined and physically passed. Combine controls only after requirements prove paired operation and measured skew is tolerated; reject this signal-relay architecture if actual motor/actuator current is required.
