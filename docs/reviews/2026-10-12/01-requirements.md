## Ranked findings

1. **A smaller relay carrier does not establish a smaller system.** If “replace the large module” is judged by carrier size alone, added supervision, wiring and controls can hide the actual packaging cost. CONFIRMED SOURCE: original module silkscreen rectangle is 133.25 × 52 mm (`capstone_Oscar_Zheng_Jared_05032026/PCB_2.0/PCBGerberFiles/8-Relay Module.kicad_mod:59–67`); carrier Edge.Cuts are 60 × 50 mm (`hardware/passive_bypass_rev_a/mila_passive_bypass_bench_rev_a.kicad_pcb:7934–7971`); supervisor Edge.Cuts are 80 × 60 mm (`hardware/passive_bypass_supervisor_rev_a/mila_passive_bypass_supervisor_rev_a.kicad_pcb:13902–13939`). ANALYTICAL INFERENCE: carrier area falls about 57%, but summed new-board area is 7,800 versus 6,929 mm², about 13% larger than the module rectangle. This compares different functions and excludes enclosure/clearance; it is not a full-system size verdict. Change: define maximum assembled volume, installation footprint, connector count and assembly/rework hours before optimization.

2. **An application requirement cannot be passed while its limit is unknown.** During loss of power, DAC collapse can precede contact return; even correct settled NC mapping may violate an endpoint's diagnostic window. CONFIRMED SOURCE: `docs/passive-bypass-validation.csv:6–8` requires endpoint error, correlation and timing limits; those procedures are NOT_RUN. `docs/passive-bypass-design.md:393–397` explicitly leaves interface, safe-state and environmental requirements open. Change: create a six-path requirements table with function, endpoints, source/load impedance, voltage/fault envelope, accepted states, channel correlation and allowed interruption/response time. Leave unavailable values UNKNOWN. Screening values cannot fill vehicle requirements.

3. **Shrinking unknown functionality risks preserving the wrong architecture.** If the lab needs capabilities already available through its supported vehicle interface, analog substitution creates unnecessary investigation; if it needs independent longitudinal control, that must be explicitly justified. CONFIRMED SOURCE: `PROJECT_CONTEXT.md:20–32` leaves responsibility unfinished and `:59–63` leaves vehicle/harness identity open. Current [upstream Nissan definitions](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/commaai/opendbc/master/opendbc/car/nissan/values.py), retrieved 2026-10-12, specify LEAF 2018–23 and ProPILOT Assist; that does not identify this vehicle or prove its support. Change: name one research experiment and the specific command authority it needs. Decide the analog board's necessity against that experiment before creating a new vehicle PCB.

4. **The useful novelty is measured power-loss transfer, not another schematic.** The current circuit already offers a concrete hypothesis: healthy nonlatching contacts restore documented connectivity without controller power. Its transition is explicitly finite (`docs/passive-bypass-design.md:11–14`), and no physical result is recorded (`:387–391`). More simulated or CAD success cannot close this uncertainty. Preserve the two-board design as a reference; obtain a comparative waveform before further integration.

## Minimum useful experiment and decision gates

PROPOSED CHANGE: characterize one actual G6K two-pole coupon with the proposed driver/cutoff/flyback and two independently bounded source pairs; compare with the existing relay module on the same isolated fixture. Use the documented 0–5 V, ≤1 mA signal envelope and external references (`docs/passive-bypass-design.md:35–53`). Capture both COMs, DAC rail and coil-current decay during engagement, release and abrupt power removal. Record open interval, bounce, pole skew, static error and source contention. This cannot validate the whole supervisor or six-path assembly.

Kill/pivot: stop on unexpected mapping/current or source short; abandon a vehicle replacement if measured waveforms exceed subsequently established endpoint limits; retain existing hardware if the complete installation misses the agreed size/effort target; pivot authority if analog substitution adds no needed experiment capability. Unknowns ranked: required research function, exact endpoint identity including K7/K8, electrical/diagnostic envelope, physical transfer behavior, complete packaging/cost baseline.
