# 18 — Adversarial reviewer

2026-10-12. Independent phase 1; source inspection only. No physical result or fresh shared validation claimed.

**Verdict: KEEP EXISTING HARDWARE for now; PROTOTYPE FIRST only as a bounded isolated experiment.** Do not purchase the complete two-board design yet. The physical question is narrower than the implemented supervisor: can this exact compact contact arrangement transfer the real signal envelope acceptably? Existing hardware is a comparison fixture, not approved vehicle hardware.

1. **Requirements can invalidate the entire selection.** Trigger: K7/K8 proves to be an actuator/polarity interface, or its intermediate/default state violates endpoint limits. Consequence: matching legacy nets with signal relays preserves an inappropriate interface rather than producing a viable redesign. CONFIRMED SOURCE: `docs/passive-bypass-design.md:27–29,100–113,393–397`; `docs/passive-bypass-validation.csv:24` leaves actual function, inductance and allowed behavior unknown. PROPOSED CHANGE: identify every endpoint and measured envelope before choosing production parts. Two same-source mixed states are connectivity facts, not proof of braking or safety.

2. **“Smaller” is not yet demonstrated at system level.** Trigger: both present modules and cable are required. Consequence: fewer relay packages can increase installation and assembly burden. CONFIRMED SOURCE: carrier outline is 60×50 mm (`hardware/passive_bypass_rev_a/mila_passive_bypass_bench_rev_a.kicad_pcb:7944–7971`); supervisor 80×60 mm (`hardware/passive_bypass_supervisor_rev_a/mila_passive_bypass_supervisor_rev_a.kicad_pcb:13912–13939`), with 64 circuit parts (`hardware/passive_bypass_supervisor_rev_a/README.md:5`). ANALYTICAL INFERENCE: summed bare-board area is 7,800 mm² versus the recorded old module footprint 133.25×52 = 6,929 mm² (`docs/dbw-switch-redesign-review.md:203`), approximately 13% larger before cable/buttons. This is not an installed-volume comparison; stacking may help. Require an enclosure drawing and actual assembly-effort estimate before crediting compactness.

3. **Fault recovery can resume without another ARM.** Trigger: one request/permission conductor opens and reconnects while returned 5 V remains valid. Consequence: coil authority resumes under retained ARM, contrary to an unqualified “manual rearm after disconnect” claim. CONFIRMED SOURCE: `tools/passive_bypass_model.py:163–175`; `docs/passive-bypass-design.md:282–288`. PROPOSED CHANGE: expose and measure this boundary; either constrain bench wiring operationally or derive connector supervision from actual application requirements. Do not add arbitrary interlocks before learning which reconnect matters.

4. **The supervisor can authorize a stale command.** Trigger: heartbeat and READY remain valid while DAC code is wrong or no longer updated. Consequence: the electronic cutoff never receives evidence of the error. CONFIRMED SOURCE: `tools/passive_bypass_model.py:1–7`; `docs/passive-bypass-fmea.md:90–93`. A faster watchdog or more heartbeat stages does not fix semantics. Test a frozen DAC with continuing WDI; budget independent output/freshness checks only after defining required authority.

5. **Successful settled bypass can conceal the decisive failure.** Trigger: DAC power collapses before NO opens, followed by contact gap and channel skew. Consequence: selected outputs are invalid before NC settles; a DC continuity test passes afterward. CONFIRMED SOURCE: `docs/passive-bypass-design.md:250–261`; model timings are supplied assumptions (`tools/passive_bypass_model.py:334–340`). [OMRON G6K datasheet](https://omronfs.omron.com/en_US/ecb/products/pdf/en-g6k.pdf), p.3 Characteristics, calls its properties initial values and requires suitability under actual operating conditions. No application transfer allowance exists yet.

**Cheapest decisive experiment:** use one exact G6K-2P-Y DC5, fitted diode/driver, two distinguishable 0–5 V sources through 10 kΩ and 100 kΩ loads. Compare both COM waveforms, DAC decay and coil current during abrupt/slow supply loss against the old relay module under identical containment. Record gap, skew, bounce and selected-dead-source duration; repeat before fabricating the supervisor.

**Kill/pivot criteria:** stop on source contention, unexpected current or non-restored NC connectivity. Reject application advancement if measured transfer/error exceeds subsequently established endpoint limits, K7/K8 requires another rating/topology, or total installed size/effort offers no defined benefit. Missing limits prevent acceptance, not characterization. A Form-B alternative needs its own overlap/error experiment; SN3257 cannot satisfy the current passive-zero-power requirement.
